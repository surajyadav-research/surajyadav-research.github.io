I am currently a Data Scientist III at S&P Global.

My research interests lie in Efficient Learning, with a focus on developing computationally efficient methods for adapting and optimizing modern machine learning models.

I completed my Master’s degree at IIIT Delhi, where I was advised by Prof. Saket Anand. Prior to that, I completed my B.Tech. from NIT Nagpur, where my bachelor’s thesis was advised by Prof. Pradip B. Damole.

Broadly, I am interested in efficient model adaptation, optimization, representation learning, and evaluation. I am particularly interested in methods that improve modern machine learning systems while considering computational cost, reliability, and practical usability.
